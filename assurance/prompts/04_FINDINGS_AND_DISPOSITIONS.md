# Reconcile GR86 findings and owner dispositions

```text
Act as custodian after sealed first-pass submissions exist. First verify all
assessment, packet and submission hashes/snapshot IDs and actual isolation
records. If no qualified independent sessions exist, say NOT_RUN/BLOCKED; a
synthetic fixture, role prompt or designer checklist is not an independent review.

Preserve each original finding and contradiction. Reconcile duplicate mechanisms
without dropping evidence or substituting votes for proof. Compare with canonical
I32 requirement/finding IDs and earlier dispositions only after first-pass reports
are sealed. Retain historic source effectivity; old digital closure is not new
physical qualification, and unsupported extraction is not automatically a defect.

For each finding record exact object/pin/net/UUID, applicable source and criterion,
conditions, mechanism/consequence, observed/expected result, reproduction/evidence,
confidence, limitations, affected gates, proposed disposition and closure evidence.
Separate defect, risk, source/coverage gap, authority conflict and preference.
Keep candidate GR86-REVIEW routes distinct from approved requirement IDs.

Request the owner's concrete dispositions and accepted repair IDs. Record who
accepted what and why; do not silently reject findings, change criteria, label
unperformed work PASS or close review/physical gates. Produce a bounded repair
proposal with source ownership, verification and rollback implications, then stop.
No source edits, supplier commitments, hardware/vehicle actions or merge.
```
