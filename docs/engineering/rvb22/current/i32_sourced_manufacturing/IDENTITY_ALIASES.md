# Explicit procurement identity mappings

The qualified design MPN remains unchanged. `SupplierMPN` and the JLC import use
the catalog spelling. These are five individually evidenced identity mappings,
not a general rule permitting similar part numbers or interchangeable suffixes.

| Design MPN | Supplier/catalog spelling | References | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| CGA4J2X7R2A104K125AA | CGA4J2X7R2A104KT0Y0U | C101, C123, C503 | [Newark exact part and “Also Known As” field](https://www.newark.com/tdk/cga4j2x7r2a104k125aa/cap-0-1uf-100v-mlcc-0805/dp/40Y2282) |
| CGA6N3X7R2A225K230AB | CGA6N3X7R2A225KT0Y0U | C103, C121, C122, C129 | [DigiKey exact part and alias field](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/tdk-corporation/CGA6N3X7R2A225K230AB/2443344) |
| CGA3E2C0G1H101J080AA | CGA3E2C0G1H101JT0Y0N | C126, C514, C524 | [DigiKey exact part and alias field](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/tdk/CGA3E2C0G1H101J080AA/2443118) |
| 0430451200 | 430451200 | F1 | [Molex product 43045-1200, including leading-zero keyword](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/part-detail/43045-1200) |
| ERA3AEB1472V | ERA-3AEB1472V | R104 | [Panasonic manufacturer identity and specifications](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/high-precision-chip-resistors/models/ERA3AEB1472V), paired with captured JLC C2074591 |

TDK's [manufacturer naming notification](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/1683658.pdf)
explains why a catalog number's suffix differs from the item description printed
on its packaging. The three exact pairings above are separately listed by the
suppliers; the naming notification alone is not used to infer any other pairing.
Sources were checked on September 15, 2026. Supplier labels and received lots
still require normal receiving inspection.
