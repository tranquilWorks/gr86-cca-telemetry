## Power domains and safe service configuration

Normal source flow is R128→+5V→R151→U152→isolated3V3_VIN/C162→U151→3V3_SOURCE→R153→main+3V3. U153 connects the main to R158/C206 only when enabled. Q152/R165 drain the source on disable; R168 drains the isolated bulk capacitor. Selected TPS22953 has no QOD, so these discharge paths are intentional source components.

GPS has its own5 V-fed MIC5504 regulator. U402 is powered from main3V3 and buffers GPS TX/PPS toward the MCU; U404 is powered from GPS3V3 and buffers MCU TX/FIX toward GPS. Their SN74LVC2G125 Ioff behavior protects the opposite unpowered domain;220 ohm series parts and100 kohm pulls remain. The oil/excitation dividers use1 Mohm/4.02 kohm ahead of the main-powered OPA2333: ordinary5 V becomes about20 mV at the unpowered input, with limited current. Do not generalize this to arbitrary externally driven programmer or sensor fault voltages. [TI buffer power-off behavior](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g125.pdf) supports the buffer-domain argument.

|---|---|

Never change isolation links while energized. Remove all sources, wait at least10 s, and measure upstream5 V, isolated VIN, source3V3, main3V3 and bulk capacitor below0.2 V before touching links. The delay covers more than five time constants of the maximum1.43208 mF reservoir with a1.06 kohm bleed; measurement remains controlling for faults or backpower. Probe ground clips must not short isolated sensor returns. Bottom probe access requires carrier removal as listed in the mechanical record.
