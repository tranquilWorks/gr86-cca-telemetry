## Adopted circuit

U152, **TPS22953QDQCRQ1**, isolates U151's input reservoir. It has reverse blocking when disabled, including loss of bias power, and **no quick output discharge**. One C162, **T598X477M010ATE025**, supplies 470 uF nominal at the isolated input. C159, **CGA4J1C0G2A333J125AE**, is a 33 nF, 100 V, 5% C0G timing capacitor. R160/R161 are 6.34 kohm/1 kohm and sense **3V3_VIN, the isolated U151 input**; R162/R163 are 4.7 kohm/1 kohm and sense the upstream 5 V rail for U152 enable. These four resistors use TNPU0603 0.02%, 2 ppm/K parts.

U152 PG, R159 47 kohm and C165 220 nF control the existing enable sequence and Q153. R166/R167, each 330 kohm, divide that PG signal for the actual U151 EN pin. The PG signal and U151 EN are distinct nets. Q153 pulls Q152's gate low while powered. When it releases, R164 pulls Q152's gate toward the isolated input; Q152 discharges the U151 **source node**, through R165, before the input can fall far below it. Q152/Q153 are **DMG2302UKQ-7**. R165 is specifically **CRCW25124R70FKEGHP**, 4.7 ohm/1.5 W, not the standard 1 W resistor. R154's 1 kohm bleed remains. An ordinary output-to-input Schottky was rejected because a typical forward drop does not bound the cold/high-current drop below 0.3 V.

U153, another **TPS22953QDQCRQ1**, charges only the C206 reservoir. The main 3.3 V rail remains directly supplied through R153. C206 is **T598X687M006ATE025**, 680 uF/6.3 V, with **WSL2512R3000FEA**, 0.30 ohm, R158 in its branch. C166 is **CGA5L1C0G2A683J160AE**,68 nF/100 V/5% C0G with soft termination. Its slower charge rate resolves the4.75 V/cold750 mA upstream current-limit collapse found in final source review. C167, **GCM188R71C105KA64D**, provides 1 uF/16 V input decoupling. R169/R170, **TNPU06033K01HWEA00 / TNPU06031K00HWEA00**, enable U153 only after its input/bias is valid. SNS senses its undivided input; PG is explicitly unconnected. R168, 1 kohm, drains the isolated reservoir after shutdown. Enabled switches conduct in both directions; the model does not turn them into ideal diodes.

U202 remains **TPS3808G33QDBVRQ1**. New R204, **CRCW0603100KFKEA**, ties CT to VDD through 100 kohm, within TI's 40-200 kohm requirement. The published 180-420 ms reset delay prevents boot from starting while C206 is still charging. C203 remains DNP. U151 feedback becomes **TNPU060311K8HWEA00 / TNPU06035K05HWEA00**, 11.8 kohm/5.05 kohm: nominal source target 3.336633663 V. Feedback remains at the regulator source node, before R153; no remote-sense claim is made. The 400 kHz FPWM selection and derivative controller coefficients are unchanged.

Input protection changes are coordinated: D101 is **S5JHE3_A/H**, 600 V; R101 is **CRCW251212K1FKEG**, 12.1 kohm/1 W; D105 is **SMCJ110AHE3_A/H**, directly across REV_BLOCKED_12V and GND. Q101 STD25NF20 and the existing controller/gate/clamp/filter topology remain. The raw clamp arrests the harness-inductance excursion before it reaches Q101's 200 V limit. The 110 V standoff keeps the defined 100 V load-dump fixture below its cold breakdown envelope.

## Alternatives and model provenance

The original 470 uF/82 milliohm network's flags motivated this work. A 330 uF/0.47 ohm intermediate improved startup but still produced an extra reset at a combined low-reference/aged-cap/slow-loop load corner. CFF 470 pF/1 nF/1.5 nF and extra main ceramics were tested; they did not supply adequate reset margin. Higher damping alone worsened the dip. A six-input-cap, 680 uF output alternative passed central cases but failed the complete positive storage stack. It is rejected, not fitted. Controlled C206 charging separates upstream startup energy from load-release storage. The 180-420 ms reset delay removes the observed boot-before-reservoir-charge mechanism. Failed runs remain in the rejected evidence index.

The downstream model is the disclosed I31 current-mode derivative (KP14.4, FZ1000 Hz, FP80000 Hz, TI3 us); the slow sensitivity halves gains/frequencies and doubles current-response time. No gains were tuned to obtain passes, no 3.6 V artificial clamp was added, and all extrema use full finite records. READY-masked minima are **not** sufficient: unmasked post-READY and explicitly timed load-window minima are retained. Intentional source-off/UV/OV resets are distinguished from normal load resets.

The unmodified TI LM5164-Q1 library SHA256 is `e11ddf6995163f98eda6205de6a7cb74c8a3982b7328467de8d806286e894b5d`. The isolated 5.5 ms baseline completed. The historical 170-part closed cascade aborted on ngspice42 at 21.3077395343 ms (14,521,189 finite samples) and ngspice47 at 21.2545563362 ms (14,463,289 finite samples), before the requested 24 ms. Alternative solver conditioning failed earlier. The records, exact decks and logs are retained; neither is a selected177-part vendor startup pass. The LM63615 encrypted model was not decoded. Agreement between timesteps only checks numerical resolution of the derivative, not its unknown controller equations.

## Final upstream-voltage correction and energy balance

U121's fitted R122309 kohm/R123100 kohm and nominal1.2 V reference give **4.908 V**. The LM5164-Q1 reference is1.181–1.218 V; the fitted0.1%/25ppm divider and ripple also contribute. Retain the existing **4.75–5.25 V interface envelope**. Earlier model5.0 V nominal/4.91–5.09 V corners were insufficient and are superseded. Neither the0.45 A sustained profile nor the0.75 A electrical/startup stress is reduced.

At4.75 V, the old C16633 nF minimum timing corner charged maximum1.43208 mF C206 at about163.36 mA before leakage/bleed. With750 mA main load, the5 V converter stayed in current limit and its rail fell; isolated VIN crossed the worst SNS falling threshold around3.675 V. Increasing input precharge delay alone merely moved the same failure later. Two input capacitors plus doubled input CT did not remove the sustained charge demand. Their failed and retrying records are retained.

The selected68 nF fastest allocated ramp limits the capacitive term to **79.35 mA**. Add8.568 mA maximum polymer leakage and about3.7 mA bleed; the extra branch demand is about91.6 mA. Using roughly0.845 A total U151 output current,3.395 V source,0.115 ohm hot winding resistance,80% conversion efficiency,4.707 V isolated input and0.396 W other5 V demand gives approximately0.88 A upstream including isolated-capacitor leakage and small control allocations. The old faster case gives roughly0.96 A by the same screen. Compare these with the declared0.95 A upstream average-current guard; the full derivative includes its finite response and actual passive network. These calculations explain the mitigation, without replacing the transient result.

Maximum C206 stores about8.25 mJ at3.395 V. The minimum210.56 uF input capacitor releases only about0.91 mJ between4.707 V and3.675 V, so extra storage alone cannot sustain the old charging-current deficit. Slower charging preserves bulk energy and spreads its demand over approximately61–104 ms. The180 ms minimum reset delay permits charging before normal boot; the slowest-charge/earliest-boot case checks the full sequence.

C166's selected body maximum is3.60 x1.90 x1.90 mm; its custom native footprint uses manufacturer-recommended reflow dimensions PA2.20/PB1.10/PC1.50 mm and a matching maximum-envelope STEP. Its final back-side center is85.85,-3.00 mm at90 degrees. Native copper-to-edge clearance is0.30 mm. The maximum body reaches0.10 mm from the nominal flange edge; supplier placement/board-edge tolerances and first-article fit must be checked together. The inherited mechanical envelope includes0.30 mm XY placement allowance and the complete1.71 mm vertical stack.
